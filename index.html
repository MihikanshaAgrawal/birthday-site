A LETTER FROM MY HEART

Happy Birthday, Meri Jaan! ♡

Sabse pehle aapko janamdin ki dher saari badhai! ❤️ Mere saath aapka 4th birthday hai aur hamesha ki tarah bahut excitement hai.

Aapke liye kya hi likhun... Aapne toh mujhe likha hai. In 4 saalon mein aapne mera jeevan bahut achha banaya. Really, you are the best yrr! ♡

Jab pehli baar woh teen shabd bole the, tab laga tha bahut time hai. Khoob enjoy karenge, masti karenge aur saath rahenge. Par ab life aise phase mein le ja rahi hai jahan kuch time ke liye bye toh bolna hi hoga.

Lekin yaad rakhna, bye ka bye nahi hona chahiye! Ek-ek pal ki update dena aur lena. Dekh lo kitni emotional hoon, abhi toh sirf message likh rahi hoon aur ismein bhi ro rahi hoon. 🥹

Long distance, long distance lagna nahi chahiye. Ye hi aapke liye aur mere liye challenge hai. ❤️

Khair, kahan ye emotional baatein karne lag gayi main! Apn aapki baat karte hain.

Aap toh kitne achhe ho, kya hi bataun! Hamesha saath diya, har phase mein saath diya. Kabhi kisi cheez ki na nahi ki mere liye. Hamesha mere liye khade rahe.

Kitni jagah gaye apn, wahan hamesha sambhala mujhe. Main har baar kuch na kuch gadbad karti hoon, uske baad bhi! 🤭

Ye 4 saal ka safar bahut hi pyaara tha ji. Aisa nahi sochna ki ye birthday last hai saath mein. Main toh aapko pata hai kaisi hoon... Duniya ke kisi bhi kone mein ho aap, aapka peecha nahi chhodungi! 💗

Library mein saath padhna, saath ghoomne jaana, scooty rides, masti, quality time... Canteen mein meri patties kha jaate ho bas! Parrr achha lagta hai. Yahi toh chhoti-chhoti memories hain apne beech.

Aur lab mein aapke saath baithna, ghar jaate ho jab dukhi hona, subah-subah jaldi aake aapka wait karna...

Woh Aghora ki sadak se nazrein na hatna jab tak aap dikh na jao. College aane ka maksad ho yrr aap toh, warna padhne kaun aata hai! 🥹❤️

Baaki bookshelf ke peeche kitni mehnat se book dhoondhte hain, pata hai na? Samajh hi gaye honge, smart ho aap toh! 🤭💗

Bas ab apne chaar saal ki memories taaza kar li. Ab aage ki kahani baad mein... ♡
